Helping students find a place to live, learn, and feel at home.

Who We Are?

EduSpace helps students find accommodation that fits their needs, budget, and campus routine. We bring useful housing details together so students can compare options and make informed choices with confidence.

Empowering Students with Safe Housing

Our goal is to make the search for student housing clearer and less stressful. We connect students with convenient places to live and the information they need to choose a home where they can focus, grow, and feel secure.
